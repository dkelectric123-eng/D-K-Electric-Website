Questions

Straight answers

How quickly can you get to Geneva?

We are based in Batavia, the next town along the Fox River, so Geneva is one of the shortest runs we make. We will give you a real time when you call rather than a promise on a web page.

Do you work on older Geneva homes?

Yes, and they are a good share of the residential work we do in the Tri-Cities. Older houses usually need specific circuits brought up to date rather than everything torn out — which one you are looking at is a look-and-see, not a phone guess.

Can you install an EV charger in Geneva?

Yes. We check the panel's capacity first, pull the permit, and run a dedicated 240V circuit.
