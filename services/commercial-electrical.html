Questions

Straight answers

Do you work with general contractors?

Yes — regularly. We are used to working to someone else's programme and coordinating with the other trades on site.

Can you work outside business hours to avoid disruption?

Often, yes. Where work would interrupt trading it is usually better done outside opening hours, and that gets planned into the quote rather than added afterwards.

Do you handle permits on commercial jobs?

Yes, permitted work is pulled and inspected as part of the job.

What size jobs do you take?

From a single unit fit-out upwards. The best way to find out whether we are the right fit is to tell us what the job is.
