Questions

Straight answers

Do you do small jobs, or only big ones?

Both. A single outlet in the right place is a perfectly normal call. There is no job too small to be worth doing properly.

My house is old. Does it need a full rewire?

Not necessarily. Plenty of older homes only need specific circuits brought up to date rather than everything torn out. Which one you are looking at depends on the wiring method, its condition and what the house is being asked to run. That is a look-and-see, not a guess over the phone.

Will you clean up afterwards?

Yes. Clean work is part of the job, not a favour.

Can you add a circuit for a hot tub or a workshop?

Yes — that is usually a dedicated circuit and sometimes a sub-panel, depending on the load and the distance.
